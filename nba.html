How to read this

Matchup grades how much this defense allows to the player's position (guards, forwards, centers): A allows the most, F the least. Early in the season it's blended with last season so a few games don't swing it.

Our proj starts from his average (blended with last season early on) and nudges it for the matchup and the game's expected pace. The +/- next to it is just the matchup and pace effect, and the list is sorted by it.

Minutes compares his last 3 games with earlier ones. Minutes drive almost every NBA prop.

How these numbers work

Player chances are Overlay's estimates from his games and the matchup; there are no sportsbook prices for NBA props here. Team-bet chances come from DraftKings' odds with the fee removed.

Sportsbooks charge a fee on every leg, and a parlay multiplies it, so a parlay's expected return is usually negative unless a leg is mispriced. Legs from the same game can be linked; books often price that in or won't allow it.
