How to read this

Win chance comes from DraftKings' moneylines with the fee removed. It's the market's number, not ours.

Overlay's rough number rates every FBS team from this season's scores, adjusted for who they played and home field. It doesn't know about injuries, weather or who's starting at quarterback. In a backtest on weeks 4–9 it was about a coin flip against DraftKings' spreads, so leans are shown for context and tracked on the Today page, not offered as edges.
