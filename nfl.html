How to read this

Hit chance is how likely the sportsbooks think the pick is to hit: a blend of up to 7 books (Pinnacle, FanDuel, DraftKings, BetMGM, ESPN Bet, BetRivers, Bovada) with their fee removed, counting the sharpest books most. "Books 73.5" under a line means the sportsbooks' average line is at least a point away from PrizePicks, which is usually where the value is. It's green when it beats what your slip type needs to break even, about 54% for a 6-pick Flex. "adj" means the book's line was a little different from PrizePicks, so Overlay adjusted it.

Our proj is Overlay's own rough estimate from a handful of games. It's a lean, not a price. When it disagrees with the hit chance, trust the hit chance.

▲ / ▼ under a line or hit chance shows how it moved since the previous odds pull (Thursday → Sunday). Lines that move usually follow injury news or sharp money.

Matchup grades how much this defense allows to the position: A allows the most, F the least. Hover or open the player for the numbers.

Usage is the last two games' pass attempts, carries or targets for that stat. ▲ or ▼ means a change of more than 15% from earlier games.

How chances are calculated

Chances come from sportsbook no-vig prices when books have the prop. Picks marked * use Overlay's rough estimate from this season's game logs and the matchup, treating picks as independent. Correlated picks hit together more often than these numbers say. Payouts vary by state and by pick type, so set the multiplier to what your app shows.

How projections work

Projections are simple estimates: a blend of season and last-3-game half-PPR scoring, nudged by the half-PPR points this defense allows to the position and by the team's implied points. Range is a typical bad week to a typical good week. Players listed Out project to 0.

Kickers and defenses use Yahoo's default scoring: field goals 3 points (0–39 yds), 4 (40–49), 5 (50+), extra points 1; defense gets 1 per sack, 2 per interception, fumble recovery, safety or blocked kick, 6 per TD, plus points-allowed tiers (+10 shutout down to −4 for 35+). A defense's projection uses its own sacks and takeaways, what the opponent's offense gives up, and the opponent's expected points. For a defense, "Team pts" is the opponent's expected points (lower is better).

Pick'em

Straight-up picks from the betting markets' win chances, tuned to your pool size. Tap a team to change a pick.

How the picks work

Win chance comes from each game's moneyline with the sportsbook fee removed. Betting markets are the most accurate free predictor of who wins.

Pool size matters. Picking every favorite gets you the most correct picks on average, but in a big pool you'll often tie with many people who did the same. Overlay simulates the week thousands of times against a pool of your size, assuming other players pick favorites a bit more often than the odds justify, and adds an upset only when it raises your chance of finishing first.

Tiebreaker uses the last game's over/under, which is the market's best guess at total points.
